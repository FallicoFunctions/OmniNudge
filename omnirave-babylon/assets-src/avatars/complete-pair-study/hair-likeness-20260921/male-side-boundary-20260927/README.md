# Male side-boundary pass

The reference has hair continuing around the sides, while the previous avatar had a high, nearly level undercut above the ear. This pass lowers the side scalp boundary behind the ear and carries the rooted fibers and attached portions of the existing long-hair cards with it. It leaves the front curls and nape geometry untouched. The cap texture's side alpha reaches opacity sooner so the lowered surface is visible; its RGB and the forehead/rear coverage are unchanged.

The final side edge dropped 12.69 mm on average across 34 edited edge vertices, with a maximum drop of 20.15 mm. `candidate-v1/` archives the earlier 8 mm prototype. The final Blender side/back/front/oblique renders and close browser side views are in this folder. The result is fuller behind the ears but **still reads as a cropped undercut**, especially at the back. More side/rear strand geometry is needed to match the reference's looser hairstyle.

`before/` is the immutable input. `native-checks.json` verifies 40 unchanged meshes, exact topology/UVs/weights/materials/transforms/colors for the three edited meshes, exact front hair, retained morph deltas, and sampled clearance over nine pose and hair-motion corners. The minimum sampled skin gap was 2.17 mm. `male-motion-validation.json` records 27 walking/running/idle motion samples. `delivery-verification.json` checks all three male GLBs, gzip round trips, and unchanged rig, outfit, animations, other materials, texture channels, UVs, weights, and colors. The standard male preview was left open after inspection, and temporary camera-probe files were removed.
